Shoot photos / E-commerce

Make the catalog dependable before you make it beautiful.

Catalog photography earns trust through repetition: the same scale, background, angle and honest product detail from one tile to the next. The creative move is deciding those rules well once.

Make it, step by stepCopy the prompts

The point of view

Consistency is part of the product information.

Choose the background hex, crop, camera height, scale and shadow before running the assortment. For an on-model catalog, add one cast and one approved stance by category.

Create batteries by item type. Tops need front, 45-degree, back and detail; footwear needs side elevation, front, rear three-quarter, overhead and stitching macro. One generic “product photography” prompt cannot see the questions a shopper asks.

Make it

From blank page to a production-ready first set.

Work small until the cast, product and photograph agree. Then make the system repeatable.

  1. 01

    Set the house frame

    Fix aspect ratio, background color, crop, camera height and shadow behavior.

  2. 02

    Build one category battery

    Make prompts around the information needed for tops, dresses, footwear, bags or jewelry.

  3. 03

    Pilot difficult SKUs

    Choose prints, black-on-black, shine, layered construction and unusual closures—not only the easiest product.

  4. 04

    QA, then batch

    Compare every output to the source for shape, color, print, scale, seams, logo and missing parts before the pack reaches the full catalog.

Prompt book

Prompts you can actually start from.

Replace the placeholder IDs with your Astria references. Keep the factual product and identity instructions; change the creative language until it sounds like your brand.

Volumetric dress packshot · 4:5

<faceid:DRESS_ID:1> dress as a high-end volumetric ghost-mannequin packshot, straight front view, natural three-dimensional body volume without a visible person or mannequin. Preserve exact neckline, sleeves, waist seam, closure, hem and fabric color. Seamless light grey background #f2f2f2, soft contact shadow, centered consistent scale, clean catalog lighting. No extra accessories, no body parts, no redesigned garment --background_color #f2f2f2

Why this worksIt separates the desired volume from any visible model and pins the catalog background.

Footwear side elevation · 4:5

<faceid:SHOE_ID:1> shoe, strict side elevation with toe pointing right and heel left, camera level with the shoe centerline. Preserve sole thickness, heel shape, stitching, panels, laces, material and logo placement. Seamless light neutral grey studio background #f2f2f2, realistic contact shadow beneath product, natural scale. No foot, no model, no extra objects, no compression or squashing.

Why this worksDirectional and camera language makes the required product view unambiguous.

On-model PDP · 3:4

<faceid:MODEL_ID:1> woman wearing <faceid:TOP_ID:1> top and <faceid:TROUSERS_ID:1> trousers. Full-length front PDP photograph, neutral comfortable stance, top untucked, trousers reach the shoes. Same crop and scale as the catalog master, plain background #f5f5f5, even softbox light, true garment color --background_color #f5f5f5

Why this worksThe same house frame can serve every SKU while the references change.

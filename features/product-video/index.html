Generate videos / Fashion & product

Approve the photograph. Then let time into it.

The fastest route to a coherent product film is not a giant video prompt. It is a still frame whose cast, product and art direction are already right—followed by one clear movement the camera can believe.

Make it, step by stepCopy the prompts

The point of view

Motion should reveal something.

A model crosses the frame so the garment drape becomes visible. A shoe turns so the heel construction appears. A bottle rotates so the label and side profile both read. “Cinematic movement” is not an action.

Keep a short film to three beats: enter, hold, leave; closed, quarter-turn, back; still water, ripple, settle. A static camera and continuous motion often preserve the product better than a prompt full of cuts and lens moves.

Make it

From blank page to a production-ready first set.

Work small until the cast, product and photograph agree. Then make the system repeatable.

  1. 01

    Approve the first frame

    Do not animate a compromise. Fix product, face, hands, light and background while they are still easy to inspect.

  2. 02

    Choose one useful motion

    Name what the movement shows about fit, volume, construction or mood.

  3. 03

    Write three time beats

    Give the beginning, middle and end a physical action. Keep camera behavior explicit and restrained.

  4. 04

    Review frame by frame

    Check product geometry, logos, hands, face and garment continuity across the full clip—not only the poster frame.

Lookbook loop · 8 seconds · 16:9

Use the approved fashion still as the first-frame reference. 0–2s: the model walks in from the left at an easy natural pace, garment moving with her body. 2–5s: she stops in the center, shifts her weight and looks just past camera so the full outfit reads. 5–8s: she walks past camera and exits right. Static camera, one continuous take, same face, body, outfit, light and location. No cuts, no garment deformation, no extra people.

Why this worksThree physical beats make an edit-friendly loop and keep the model from improvising a new commercial.

Product turntable · 8 seconds · 1:1

The referenced shoe rotates slowly and smoothly through one full 360-degree turn over 8 seconds on a fixed studio turntable. 0s strict side view, 2s front three-quarter, 4s opposite side, 6s rear three-quarter, 8s returns exactly to starting side view. Static level camera, light grey seamless background, contact shadow stays fixed. Preserve sole, heel, stitching, panels, material and logo. No foot, no hands, no shape change, no fast spin.

Why this worksAngle checkpoints give the rotation a measurable job and make review straightforward.

Quiet campaign motion · 6 seconds · 9:16

Use the approved beauty still as the first frame. The bottle remains perfectly still and label-forward. A narrow ribbon of water enters from frame left, circles the base once, then settles into small ripples. Camera locked, reflections and lighting unchanged, luxurious restrained pace. Preserve bottle geometry, cap, label typography and color. No camera move, no text change, no extra objects.

Why this worksThe product is the anchor; motion belongs to the environment.

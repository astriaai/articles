## Fidelity review

Nano Banana 2 passes the article-size visual gate and keeps all three references legible. The output maintains Sloane's face, the dress color and broad construction, and the bag's circular gold texture. The large accessory treatment makes it especially easy to compare against the source.

The model changes individual bag discs, chain placement, pleat structure, and belt geometry. Those differences matter for a PDP or catalog asset even when the campaign frame is attractive.

## A practical Nano Banana workflow

1. Start with the complete identity, garment, and accessory reference set.
2. Keep the prompt and canvas fixed when comparing another endpoint.
3. Review the only output before deciding whether more sampling is justified.
4. Score identity, garment construction, and accessory geometry separately.
5. Retain the full-resolution download and publish a clearly labeled web derivative.

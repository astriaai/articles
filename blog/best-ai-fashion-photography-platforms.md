## First, work out which category you are buying

| Category | The job | Buy it if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Production systems** | Run a whole collection across catalog, lookbook, campaign, social, and motion from one approved direction | You produce seasons, not one-off images |
| **Virtual try-on** | Put a garment on a body — brand-side or shopper-side | Try-on specifically is the bottleneck |
| **Listing tools** | Cheap on-model images for marketplace grids | You are a seller, and listings are the whole job |
| **Enterprise and managed** | Delivered output, contractual SLAs, procurement-friendly | You need someone accountable, not a tool |
| **Design-stage** | Ideation, 3D fit, pre-production visualisation | The work happens before there is a product to photograph |

Nearly every disappointing purchase in this space is a category error rather than a product failure — a brand buying a listing tool and expecting a lookbook, or buying a design tool and expecting catalog throughput.

## Production systems

For brands producing collections. The distinguishing question: can an approved creative direction be stored and re-run against new products, by more than one person?

**Astria — our recommendation for collection-scale production.** A brand workspace holds products, casting, references, templates, and outputs, with the approved treatment — casting, styling, scene, crop, lighting, format — stored and reused rather than rebuilt per shoot. Category templates cover apparel, beauty, jewelry, footwear, sports, luxury, and editorial; casting spans ages and body types including children and extended sizes; the same direction extends into campaign frames, social crops, and motion. A multiplayer canvas with named cursors and live voice means the stylist, founder, ecommerce lead, and agency review the same work. Photographers and studios use it to install workflows for brands and keep the client relationship. **Trade-off:** it expects a setup investment. For a single listing image today, that is the wrong purchase.

## How to run the evaluation

Feature lists do not discriminate between these products; two tests do.

**Test one — your hardest garments.** Ten pieces with directional prints, sheer panels, fine hardware, and complex drape. Not your bestsellers. A tool evaluated on easy garments tells you nothing about your catalog.

**Test two — change your mind.** Get five products approved, then add a colorway and change the crop convention. Count the operations each platform needs. This is the single best predictor of running cost, and it is the test vendors do not design their demos around.

Then ask the four questions in the [AI fashion photoshoot guide](./ai-fashion-photoshoot-guide.md): can direction be stored and reused, what happens when you change your mind, can several people work on it, and what do you own at the end.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI fashion photography platform?

It depends on which of five different jobs you are buying for. For brands producing a full collection across catalog, lookbook, campaign, and social, a production system is the right category and Astria is our recommendation. For a marketplace seller who needs cheap listing images, a listing tool is sufficient and cheaper.

### Are these tools interchangeable?

No, and treating them as interchangeable is the most common buying error. A shopper-facing try-on widget, a managed retouching service, and a collection production system solve unrelated problems despite sharing the phrase "AI fashion".

### What should I test during an evaluation?

Run your ten hardest garments, not your easiest. Then change something after approval — add a colorway or change the crop convention — and count the operations required. That second test predicts your real running cost better than any feature list.

### How much do AI fashion platforms cost?

Listing tools commonly run low monthly subscriptions. Production platforms are typically usage-based. Enterprise and managed services are quoted per engagement. Compare on total cost per approved published image rather than headline price.

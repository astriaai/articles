## Fidelity review

The output passes both publication gates. It looks clean at article size and keeps the central source relationships: Sloane's face remains recognizable, the dress stays pale yellow with a sleeveless V-neck, two front buttons, belt, and long skirt, and the gold bag is not merged into the garment.

The result still interprets rather than reproduces. Pleats and belt geometry shift; some bag discs and chain details change; and the accessory occupies less of the frame than in the strongest accessory-led result. That makes this a strong campaign image, not automatic catalog evidence.

## GPT Image 2 or GPT Image 2.5?

In this shared brief, GPT Image 2 remains competitive. GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst pushes the diagonal composition harder, while Flare gives the accessory more room. The useful workflow is to keep the same source set and run the compatible variants side by side rather than infer an upgrade from the name alone.

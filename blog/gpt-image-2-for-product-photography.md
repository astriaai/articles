## GPT Image 2 at a glance

| Best fit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Beauty and cosmetics | Polished skin, lighting, product placement, and advertising finish |
| Prominent text | Correct large headline and strong designed composition in our test |
| Packaging-led concepts | Understands the relationship between portrait, pack, copy, and negative space |
| General fashion | Highly capable, although Nano Banana 2 remains our more balanced default |

The benchmark tested 11 matched briefs and requested three GPT Image 2 outputs per brief. All 33 returned. We display output `00` from every cell, fixed before review.

## Why beauty and text are one problem

Beauty advertising asks several systems to work together: believable skin, controlled reflections, recognizable packaging, exact copy, and a composition with visual hierarchy. A model can succeed at four and still make the asset unusable.

<figure className="benchmark-card benchmark-card--single"><img loading="lazy" src="/articles/img/model-benchmarks/2026-09/beauty-gpt-image-2.webp" alt="GPT Image 2 beauty campaign benchmark with serum bottle and headline" /><figcaption><strong>Beauty and text</strong>Our strongest combined beauty-ad composition with the exact large headline.</figcaption></figure>

GPT Image 2 handled the requested **COLOR, KEPT TRUE** headline, portrait finish, held product, and open-space layout especially well. That does not eliminate packaging QA. Small text can look plausible while changing a character, quantity, or hierarchy.

Use it first for:

- cosmetic campaign concepts;
- product-and-person advertising;
- packaging, signage, and large headline work;
- frames where the copy must feel integrated rather than overlaid afterward;
- premium commercial compositions that need a designed finish.

## Fashion performance

GPT Image 2 also produced one of the strongest general-fashion frames.

Why is it not the default? A default is judged across the complete workflow. Nano Banana 2 offered the more balanced route through ordinary garment, identity, movement, and reference tasks. Choose GPT Image 2 when the desired finish is closer to an advertisement or when text is a primary approval criterion.

## Prompting GPT Image 2 for commercial work

Structure the brief around the deliverable:

> Create a [campaign/PDP/editorial] image for [purpose]. Use `<person>` and `<product>` in these roles. Preserve [pack geometry, label, color, and identity]. Add the exact headline “[copy]” once in [location]. Reserve [negative space]. Use [lighting and palette]. No other text or products.

Then review four layers independently:

1. Is the required copy exact?
2. Is the real product still the same product?
3. Is the person or cast consistent?
4. Does the overall image meet the creative brief?

## Bottom line

GPT Image 2 is the model we would open first for beauty, packaging-led advertising, or prominent copy. It is also a strong fashion model, but its specialist advantage is the ability to make the frame feel designed. Never let polished typography distract from a changed label or product.

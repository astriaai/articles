**Astria Workshops · Series 2: Reusable lookbook templates · Lesson 2**

Turn a lookbook you like into a template your team can reuse with another product. In this lesson, save the prompts behind five shirt images and name the template **Shirt**.

Follow [workshop 2.2](https://www.astria.ai/academy/watch/academy-2-2/). The screenshots show its recorded steps, cropped to keep the relevant controls readable. Keyframe times are relative to the named scene. Click any image to enlarge it.

<!-- truncate -->

## Before you begin

Open the workspace containing your chosen lookbook images on [Astria Generate](https://www.astria.ai/prompts). A prompt is the instruction used to generate an image. You’ll collect those instructions into a template, so first choose a set that represents the creative direction you want to repeat.

The [previous workshop](./reuse-lookbook-creative-direction-templates.md) explains what a template carries into the next product shoot.

## Step 1: Review the five shots

Start with the front, back and side images you created, plus a close-up detail and a styled look. Together, these give the shirt a useful set of views. Check the images before saving their prompts: you’ll be repeating the decisions behind these shots.

## Step 2: Select each prompt

Hover over the first image thumbnail. A selection circle appears at its upper-left corner. Click it, then select the remaining four prompts. Each selected thumbnail has a blue check mark.

## Step 3: Click Create template

Once the set is selected, a toolbar appears at the bottom of the page. Confirm that **5 images selected** is shown, then click **Create template**. Wait for Astria to finish adding all five prompts.

## Step 4: Name the template Shirt

When the saved template opens, use the edit control beside its name to rename it **Shirt**. Naming it after the garment category makes its role clear when you choose a template for another product.

## Check your result

Confirm that the template includes the front, back, side, detail and styled prompts. It should now be ready to use with another shirt.

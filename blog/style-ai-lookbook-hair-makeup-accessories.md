**Astria Workshops · Series 1: Your first AI lookbook · Lesson 6**

Style your outfit so the clothes, hair, makeup, accessories and shoes work together. In this lesson, you’ll identify the brand’s styling choices, describe the details you want and compare the styled image with your first generation.

Follow [workshop 1.6: Styling](https://www.astria.ai/academy/watch/academy-1-6/). The examples and instructions follow the video’s order. Click a screenshot or source image to enlarge it.

<!-- truncate -->

## Before you begin

Start with your outfit on [Astria Generate](https://www.astria.ai/prompts) and the [background and lighting direction](./control-ai-lookbook-background-lighting.md) you chose in the previous lesson. Keep the brand’s product photos and your first generation available for comparison.

## Step 1: Study the hair and makeup

Look at Bobo Choses’ photos, the brand example used throughout this series. Is the hair pulled back or worn over the shoulders? Does the makeup look clean and minimal? Choose the details that fit your brand.

## Step 2: Choose accessories and shoes

Choose shoes that suit the outfit. Consider a bag or earrings when they belong in the look. In Lookbook, use **Footwear** for shoes, **Bags & Belts** for a bag and **Accessories** for earrings.

The bag and earrings in the workshop are creative styling choices for this outfit. They are not presented as Bobo Choses products or as details taken from its reference photo.

Look at the **full image** while deciding. Check how the pieces work with the clothes, hair and makeup, including the shoes at the bottom of the frame.

## Step 3: Compare the first and styled results

Put the first generation beside the result after your styling direction. Review the hair, makeup, accessories and shoes together. Notice which changes bring the look closer to the brand’s style, then check that the garment details are still correct.

## Check your result

Choose the styling you want to carry through the rest of the lookbook. Confirm that the full outfit works together and the products remain recognizable. The next lesson uses that established look to choose poses and framing that show the garment’s fit and details.

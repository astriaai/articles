**Astria Workshops · Series 2: Reusable lookbook templates · Lesson 5**

Create a designated template for each garment category you regularly photograph. Start each product from poses, framing and prompts suited to the clothes.

Follow [workshop 2.5](https://www.astria.ai/academy/watch/academy-2-5/). The screenshots show its recorded steps, cropped to keep the relevant controls readable. Keyframe times are relative to the named scene. Click any image to enlarge it.

## Before you begin

Use the reusable workflow from [workshop 2.4](./generate-multiple-styled-looks-astria.md). Think about the garment categories in your collection and the product views each one needs.

## Step 1: Match the shots to the garment

Choose poses, framing and prompts—the written instructions for each image—that suit the product. A dress may need a full-length image to show its shape. A shirt may need a closer crop around its collar.

Use those differences to decide what belongs in each category’s template. Keep the product’s important features visible in the selected views.

## Step 2: Include garment-specific styling

Save styling directions that you expect to reuse with the category. For a jacket, specify whether it should be shown **open or closed**, alongside the chosen poses and framing.

These directions are part of the template’s creative decisions. Review them with the shot list before reusing the template for another product.

## Step 3: Browse an established workspace’s templates

In Astria, open **Templates**. Choose a workspace if necessary, then look through its catalog.

## Step 4: Choose between a brief and a category template

Use a **brief** when you want a change for just one generation, as in [workshop 2.3](./run-astria-template-new-product.md). When you regularly photograph dresses, shirts and jackets differently, save a template for each category.

That lets you choose the appropriate saved shot set when you start a product, instead of adapting the same template every time.

## Check your result

For each category, confirm that the template’s poses, framing and styling instructions show the garment clearly. Check the selected template before adding your next product references.

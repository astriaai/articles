| Vary the pose | Repose / Pose Changer | The same approved casting and garments in the requested shot |

## Modelia review: outfit assembly and pose variation

Modelia's [platform](https://modelia.ai/) presents flatlay-to-model, mannequin-to-model, outfit creation, video and accessory tools. Its [Outfit Generator](https://modelia.ai/outfit-generator) describes combining tops, bottoms and accessories with model customization and different backgrounds.

The [Pose Changer](https://modelia.ai/pose-changer) starts from an image and a selected or described pose. Modelia says it aims to preserve identity and styling while changing the pose or camera perspective. Treat that as a feature to test with your collection, especially where the new view reveals details hidden in the original.

Modelia also publishes a [Shopify integration](https://modelia.ai/integration-shopify) for generating image sets and video reels from product photos, publishing to product pages, and reusing saved models. Store delivery belongs in the evaluation alongside styling. We have reviewed that published workflow, not tested the app or its publishing behavior.

For an ecommerce stylist, those workflows fit an immediate need: show a coordinated look, then build useful variations. The harder question is whether every product remains a faithful representation through the sequence.

## Astria: keep the collection treatment reusable

Astria's [fashion offering](https://www.astria.ai/ecommerce) uses product references and creative templates. The [workspaces gallery](https://www.astria.ai/gallery/workspaces) is a useful place to inspect how an approved direction can read as a collection.

Include Astria when a creative team wants to establish styling, casting and scene conventions, then reuse them for future looks. Ask the operator to distinguish the products that must match exact references from generic supporting styling the brief permits.

Neither platform should receive credit for a beautiful look that changes the item being sold. Separate style approval from product approval from the start.

The practical distinction to test is how much approved work survives the next request. Build a standing catalog shot, reuse its casting and product references for a campaign frame, then give the next outfit to a colleague. In Astria, make the saved template the handoff deliverable. In Modelia, ask the operator to demonstrate the equivalent reuse path, including its saved models. Record what carries forward and what must be entered again; a feature list alone does not establish the difference.

## A revealing pilot: change one item in a layered look

Choose a jacket, top, skirt or trousers, shoes and bag. Supply separate photographs for each product and keep the reference pack identical across platforms. Include a second colorway for one item.

1. **Make the SKU map.** List every product that must be visible, its reference files and any permitted occlusion.
2. **Create the first standing look.** Approve identity, styling and scene only after checking each item.
3. **Request a seated and a walking variation.** Inspect hems, sleeves, bag straps, soles and overlap between layers.
4. **Replace one colorway.** Keep every other SKU, casting and treatment fixed.
5. **Make a wider editorial image.** Check that the brand direction holds while product visibility changes.
6. **Hand off the next outfit.** Measure how much the second operator must rebuild.

The replacement step matters. If changing the top also changes the shoes or bag, record the repair effort even if the image remains attractive. A styling workflow should let the team understand what changed and why.

For source preparation, use the [flatlay-to-model guide](./flat-lay-to-on-model.md). For collection sequencing, use the [AI lookbook guide](./ai-lookbook-guide.md).

## Evaluate pose variation against the available evidence

A new pose can expose an unseen side seam or the underside of an accessory. Add source photographs for those details when they matter to the brief. If they are unavailable, keep the shot within the supported view or request more photography.

Review accessory geometry as carefully as the garment. Check the number and position of straps, closure shape, shoe panels and visible branding. A model's face can remain convincing while the products quietly change.

## Which workflow should you choose?

**Start with Modelia** when outfit assembly, accessories and pose variations are the immediate tasks. Its published tools make those relevant pilot categories; the outcome on your garments should determine adoption.

**Choose Astria for the collection workflow** when you want a reusable creative treatment and a specialist-to-brand handoff. Require the same item-level accuracy and pose tests rather than assuming the template will handle every look equally well.

If both pass, compare the work needed for the second outfit and later product replacement. That is more informative than selecting the best single generated image.

## Pricing and the cost of revisions

Check [Modelia pricing](https://modelia.ai/pricing) and [Astria pricing](https://www.astria.ai/pricing). Scope the intended images, video, resolution and production access directly with the vendor where needed.

Estimate a complete styled set, including pose retries, accessory corrections and human review. Use accepted looks and accepted SKU coverage as the denominator. An output that omits a required bag is not a completed look merely because the rest of the outfit passes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Astria better than Modelia for a fashion collection?

For a brand that needs a reusable creative treatment across outfits, colorways and campaigns, Astria is our recommendation. Modelia also publishes outfit, pose, saved-model and Shopify workflows. Run the same product-fidelity and handoff pilot before choosing; we have not measured a quality advantage.

### Can Modelia create complete outfits?

Yes. Modelia publishes an outfit generator combining garments and accessories on digital models, with model and background customization. Check each item against its own product reference.

### Can Modelia change a model pose from an image?

Yes. Its pose changer accepts an image and a selected or described pose. Preserving identity and clothing is a published aim that still needs checking on your inputs.

### Does a convincing styled outfit prove that every product is accurate?

No. A coherent overall look can conceal a wrong accessory, altered fastening or invented detail. Approve each SKU separately, then review the outfit and collection together.

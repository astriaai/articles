# Content refresh ledger

Every published article carries a date and, in the comparisons, a "Reviewed <date>" line
and a `(2026)` title. Those are assets while they are true and liabilities the moment they
are not. This file is the review schedule.

**Publish dates:** all posts were re-dated on 2026-08-23 to run Aug 1–23 rather than into
the future. Docusaurus renders future-dated posts immediately, so dating ahead published
everything at once with future timestamps. Each comparison's in-body "Reviewed" and
"available on" lines were moved to match its new publish date; the underlying research was
actually done 2026-08-21/22, which is what the "Last fact check" column records.

**Process:** review monthly. For each row past its next-review date, re-verify the volatile
claims listed, update the article and its `Reviewed` line, then set a new next-review date.
Comparisons carry the highest risk because competitor pricing and feature scope move fastest.

**Volatile claim types**
- `P` pricing, plans, tiers
- `F` feature scope, product names, integrations
- `S` vendor-published statistics and customer names
- `M` model availability and model names
- `L` legal/regulatory (disclosure rules for synthetic imagery)

## Open items

- **Cover images.** New articles ship without `image:` frontmatter, so the index renders a
  letter card and social shares fall back to the site-wide default. Covers belong in
  `static/img/covers/<slug>.jpg` in the lilac-editorial series. Never place assets under a
  path containing `blog` — the www.astria.ai proxy 403s any `/articles/` URL containing it.
- **Search Console.** Confirm `/articles/` is covered by the www.astria.ai property, submit
  `/articles/sitemap.xml`, and verify the proxy is not blocking Googlebot on article paths.
- **Alternatives cluster.** `botika-alternatives` is the pilot. Extend to other competitors
  only where query data shows the "<competitor> alternatives" intent is distinct from the
  "astria vs <competitor>" page already ranking.
- **Astria vs FLAM.** Deliberately not written. Founder history makes it a leadership call.

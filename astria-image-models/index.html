Which AI Image Model Should You Use in Astria? (2026 Guide)

September 7, 2026 · 7 min read
Astria Team
AI fashion production

Start with GPT Image 2.5 when you want a polished, reference-led fashion campaign frame. Sunburst is the more aggressive editorial variant; Flare gives the garment and accessory more room. GPT Image 2, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5 Pro, Muse, and MAI Image remain useful routes when their visible tradeoffs fit the job.

The guidance below comes from one controlled Sloane/dress/bag brief. It is deliberately narrower than the earlier mixed-source scorecard, which has been retired from the public article family.

A simple routing workflow​

  1. Choose the model whose visible treatment best matches the job.
  2. Run the actual identity, garment, and accessory references together.
  3. Keep prompt, aspect ratio, resolution policy, and output count fixed for a comparison.
  4. Review the original downloads for identity, garment construction, and accessory geometry.
  5. Treat web derivatives as presentation assets, not as proof of master resolution.
  6. Approve a setup before multiplying it across a collection.

Bottom line​

GPT Image 2.5 is the headline route in the current article family because both variants delivered strong, high-resolution, reference-led fashion frames. The other endpoints remain useful when their specific composition, accessory treatment, canvas behavior, or native resolution fits the production need.

How to Review Your AI Lookbook Before Creating a Template

October 9, 2026 · 3 min read
Topics
  • Guides

Astria Workshops · Series 1: Your first AI lookbook · Lesson 9

Review the lookbook as a set before making it reusable. The brand’s identity comes through the creative decisions you have chosen: styling, hair, accessories and lighting.

This short review follows workshop 1.9: Lookbook Review, the final lesson in Series 1.

Before you begin​

Gather the selected images from your pose and framing work. Keep the original product references available for checking the clothes.

Step 1: Look at the finished images together​

Review the full set. Are you happy with the styling, lighting and how the clothes are shown? Does it feel consistent and represent the brand the way you intended?

Step 2: Decide whether the look is ready to reuse​

Check the styling, hair and accessories, the light, and the way each image shows the garment. Compare the clothes with the original product photos. Resolve any result you would not want to carry into more product images.

Step 3: Continue to reusable templates​

Once you’re happy with the set, you can scale up by turning it into a reusable template. Series 2 explains how to use that template to create images for more products.

At this point, you have reviewed the lookbook. Creating and running the template comes next.

The complete input reference set​

The product sources pass the 1600px long-edge gate. Sloane's 679×722 portrait is the disclosed exception and was never upscaled. Identity observations are therefore directional; garment and accessory observations use the higher-resolution masters.

Semantic prompt​

Dress hero shot. An off-kilter, flash-lit photograph of the referenced woman in a dynamic, almost off-balance leaning pose against a dark wooden bar counter or pillar at night. Her body creates a diagonal line. She wears the reference dress and holds the reference bag. The strong flash casts a sharp shadow. Blurred shelves with bottles and bar lights define the out-of-focus background. Cool, edgy vibe.

The generation requests use the exact Articles-workspace tune tokens. There is no editorial prefix and no text-to-image substitute.

GPT Image 2.5: Sunburst and Flare​

Sunburst is the more forceful editorial treatment. Flare makes the accessory easier to approve in context. Both pass the article-size visual gate and both still require product-detail review.

The same brief in six other routes​

What this comparison can and cannot decide​

The comparison supports a few narrow observations:

  • Sunburst pushes the diagonal, flash-led editorial direction furthest.
  • Flare and Seedream keep the complete look easy to read.
  • Nano Banana 2 gives the bag the strongest visual presence.
  • GPT Image 2 remains highly competitive on direction and garment handling.
  • Muse combines the references well but misses the requested aspect ratio.
  • Both MAI variants now complete the reusable three-reference workflow, at lower native output resolution in this test.

It does not prove a universal ranking. One output cannot estimate retry rates, and the low-resolution Sloane source limits identity conclusions. Every model changes some pleats, belt geometry, chain placement, or individual bag discs.

Production recommendation​

Start with the model whose visible tradeoff matches the job. Use Sunburst for aggressive editorial energy, Flare for product-complete GPT Image 2.5 framing, Nano Banana 2 when accessory presence matters, Seedream for a complete 2K route, Muse when reference separation matters more than exact canvas shape, and MAI only after its native resolution is sufficient for the destination.

Then rerun the actual SKU and identity. Keep the source set, semantic prompt, aspect ratio, and output count fixed, and review the original download rather than a web derivative.

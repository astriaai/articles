Best AI Image Models for Fashion and Product Photography (2026 Benchmark)

September 6, 2026 · 21 min read
Astria Team
AI fashion production

Nano Banana 2 is the best general choice for fashion. GPT Image 2 is our first specialist choice for beauty and text. Seedream 5 is the practical choice for swimwear, rich color, difficult texture, and strong identity preservation. Muse is the reference-first model for jewelry and briefs with several simultaneous constraints.

That is the result of 11 matched briefs, four core models, and 131 returned images—not a generic ranking copied from a public leaderboard. A new matched Sloane follow-up adds GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Flare without rewriting the original scorecard.

How to read the grids​

The order is fixed throughout: Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Seedream 5 Pro, Muse. Every card shows output 00, selected before viewing the results. Three images were requested per model and brief, and the other outputs were retained rather than used to pick a prettier winner.

All references are synthetic and brand-neutral. Portrait comparisons used 3:4 because it was supported by all four endpoints; jewelry used 1:1. Read the complete benchmark methodology and disclosure.

1. General fashion: Nano Banana 2 is the default​

Brief: one fixed adult identity, one referenced white tank, ivory wide-leg trousers, black slingbacks, a walking pose, pale-stone gallery, late-afternoon side light, and no text or extra people.

All four models produced usable fashion frames. Nano Banana 2 earns the default recommendation from its balance across this and the other fashion tasks, not from a runaway win in one image.

This is the most important result because it reflects ordinary production. A default should be broadly competent before it is exceptional. Nano Banana 2 followed the art direction, preserved the cast and shirt, gave the fabric believable movement, and produced a composition that did not demand a new workflow decision.

2. Difficult texture and color: Seedream 5 stands out​

Brief: preserve the synthetic jacket's burnt-orange jacquard, teal crane motifs, brass buttons, collar, sleeves, and scalloped hem over a black dress against deep teal.

The source jacket is synthetic. Ground truth follows the rendered source: four crane motifs and four brass buttons.

Seedream 5's advantage is easiest to see rather than reduce to a single score: saturated color remains rich without becoming flat, and the fabric reads as a woven, light-reactive surface instead of an orange print pasted onto clothing. It also keeps the cast identity convincing under harder studio lighting.

3. Swimwear: all four completed; Seedream is the practical fallback​

Two tasteful briefs tested the same adult identity and cobalt swimsuit: straight-on ecommerce and a calm poolside editorial. Both specified ordinary commercial framing, exact strap and ring details, and no provocative pose.

Ecommerce​

Seedream preserved the product well but its predetermined first ecommerce output did not satisfy the requested full-length framing.

Poolside editorial​

Every endpoint produced safe, tasteful outputs. The Nano Banana 2 request returned two images instead of three, with no reported error or moderation reason; we do not claim to know why.

The measured run does not establish a moderation-rate ranking. It does support Seedream 5 as a strong production choice for the category: it completed both briefs, kept the unusual cobalt/coral relationship and asymmetric straps recognizable, and preserved the cast well. In broader use, that makes it the route to try when an otherwise benign adult swimwear job encounters stricter sensitivity handling.

4. Beauty and exact text: GPT Image 2 is the specialist​

Brief: close beauty portrait, a referenced rose serum bottle held beside the face, exact bottle label, and the headline COLOR, KEPT TRUE once above the left shoulder.

The large headline is readable in all four selected outputs. The specialist verdict combines typography with portrait finish, product placement, and advertising composition.

GPT Image 2 is not the only model that can render text—the grid makes that obvious. It is our first choice here because the entire frame behaves like finished beauty advertising. The portrait, bottle, headline, negative space, and restrained palette work together.

Small packaging copy remains a high-risk detail in every generated image. Use the generated frame as campaign creative, but verify the pack against the real product and replace critical legal or product copy in post when exactness is required.

5. Jewelry: Muse is the reference-first choice​

Brief: place the same nine-emerald, rose-gold necklace on the cast; preserve the settings, small white diamonds, chain, spacing, and proportions; frame both face and necklace clearly.

A convincing necklace is not necessarily the same necklace. Compare stone count, prongs, spacing, chain, clasp, scale, and placement before commerce use.

Muse earns the reference-first recommendation because it treats the jewelry as a product constraint rather than a general aesthetic suggestion. That is also why it is the model we would add when other outputs look excellent but repeatedly simplify the referenced object.

For primary listing imagery, keep a macro photograph of the actual piece. The wider decision framework is in AI jewelry photography.

All four rows keep a recognizable cast. Seedream 5 and Muse are particularly strong reference options; Nano Banana 2 remains the balanced fashion default across the complete set.

Identity is not just a face crop. A production model has to survive changing camera distance, body visibility, pose, and fabric movement. This test is deliberately small, so the conclusion is qualitative: all four are viable; Seedream 5's skin and facial continuity reinforce its specialist role, while Muse's consistent interpretation reinforces its reference-first role.

7. The multi-reference stress test: why Muse matters​

The final brief combines the cast, orange jacket, emerald necklace, serum bottle, exact right-hand placement, deep teal backdrop, coral floor strip, product attributes, and a no-extras list.

The first outputs are unusually strong across the board. Muse's recommendation is about dependable reference-led behavior across the jewelry and complex tests, not a claim that the other three failed this frame.

This is what “thinking model” means in our Muse recommendation: the output behaves as if the brief's references and constraints were considered together before composition. We are describing observable behavior, not asserting undocumented internal architecture.

This was an Astria-run editorial benchmark of endpoints available through Astria on September 6, 2026. Astria sells access to the tested models. The review was not blinded or independently funded, and three outputs per cell are not enough for statistical ranking. Model behavior, names, pricing, and availability will change.

The value of the comparison is that the prompts, display rule, output counts, prompt IDs, and visual evidence are inspectable. The verdict is intentionally practical:

Use Nano Banana 2 as the general fashion default.

Route beauty and copy-led work to GPT Image 2; route swimwear, saturated color, difficult texture, and identity-sensitive work to Seedream 5; add Muse when reference fidelity and simultaneous constraints are the hard part.
